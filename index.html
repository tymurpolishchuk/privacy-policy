Apple, the App Store, iOS, Game Center, crash reporting, analytics, or other platform services may process limited technical information according to the user's device settings and Apple's privacy policies. This information is handled by those services and is not sold by the developer.

Tracking and User Controls

The developer does not sell user data. Unity Ads may request permission to use tracking or the device advertising identifier where required by iOS. Users can allow or deny tracking permission and can manage advertising permissions in iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the Application, please contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

Last Outpost: Tower Defense Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Last Outpost: Tower Defense for iPhone, developed by Polishchuk Tymur.

Information handled by the game

The release game does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It has no user accounts, advertising, tracking, in-app purchases, or developer-operated analytics service. It does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, or location.

Local progress and preferences

Completed levels, difficulty records, language, and sound preferences are saved locally on your device. The developer does not provide cloud synchronization. An unfinished battle is kept in memory while the app remains running and is not restored after termination.

Apple services and TestFlight

Apple may process App Store transactions, system diagnostics, or TestFlight feedback according to your device settings and Apple's policies. If you submit feedback, Apple may share it and diagnostic reports with the developer. See Apple's privacy policy.

Support and contact

If you email the developer, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. For support or privacy questions, contact timurpolishchukwork@gmail.com.
