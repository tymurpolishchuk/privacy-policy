Chest Simulator: Collect Them! Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Chest Simulator: Collect Them! (the "Application"), previously named Infinity Project: open boxes. This page explains its privacy practices.

Information Collected by the Developer

The Application does not require a developer account. Gameplay progress is stored locally on the device. Advertising and platform services may process the data described below. If you contact support, the developer receives the email address and information that you choose to send in order to respond to your request.

Advertising

The Application uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation and Unity Ads to show advertisements. These advertising services may collect and process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, app interaction events, ad views, approximate location derived from IP address, diagnostics, and other technical information needed to deliver ads, prevent fraud, measure performance, and improve advertising services.

Unity may use this information according to its own privacy policy. You can learn more in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Device, Platform, and Store Services

Apple, the App Store, iOS, Game Center, crash reporting, analytics, or other platform services may process limited technical information according to the user's device settings and Apple's privacy policies. This information is handled by those services and is not sold by the developer.

Tracking and User Controls

The developer does not sell user data. iOS controls permission for tracking and use of the device advertising identifier where required by iOS. Users can allow or deny tracking permission and can manage advertising permissions in iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the Application, please contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email timurpolishchukwork@gmail.com. For gameplay help, visit Chest Simulator Support. When you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are processed to respond to your request.
