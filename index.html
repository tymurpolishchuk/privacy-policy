Infinity Project: open boxes Privacy Policy

A clear summary of how privacy is handled in the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Infinity Project: open boxes (the "Application"). By using the Application, you agree to the practices described below.

Information Collected by the Developer

The developer does not directly collect, store, sell, or share personal data from users of the Application. The Application does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to the developer.

Advertising

The Application uses Unity Ads to show advertisements. Unity Ads may collect and process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, app interaction events, ad views, approximate location derived from IP address, diagnostics, and other technical information needed to deliver ads, prevent fraud, measure performance, and improve advertising services.

Unity may use this information according to its own privacy policy. You can learn more in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Device, Platform, and Store Services

Apple, the App Store, iOS, Game Center, crash reporting, analytics, or other platform services may process limited technical information according to the user's device settings and Apple's privacy policies. This information is handled by those services and is not sold by the developer.

Tracking and User Controls

The developer does not sell user data. Unity Ads may request permission to use tracking or the device advertising identifier where required by iOS. Users can allow or deny tracking permission and can manage advertising permissions in iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the Application, please contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

For privacy questions about the Application, please contact the developer through the App Store support link for Infinity Project: open boxes.
