Reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help explain a visual issue.

Rewards and progress

Characters and currencies belong only to this simulator. They cannot be transferred to Brawl Stars or redeemed for money. Progress is saved locally on your device; deleting the app or resetting its data may remove that progress.

Advertising

Advertisements require an internet connection and may be unavailable. Contact support if an ad finishes but the expected in-game reward is not granted.
